PROJECT 01 · POWER BI

Country Development & Economic Performance Analysis

An interactive comparison of economic, demographic, health and development indicators across countries and regions.

Power BIPower QueryDAXData Visualization

Project objective

Transform country-level data into interactive views that help users compare countries, explore regional differences and understand patterns across economic and development indicators.

Preparation & analytical approach

  • Clean and transform source data using Power Query.
  • Address missing values and inconsistent country or region names.
  • Explore GDP, GDP growth, inflation, population, population density and life expectancy.
  • Develop KPI cards, country and regional comparisons, and dedicated analytical report pages.

Dashboard highlights

  • Average GDP and GDP growth indicators.
  • Life expectancy and population overview.
  • Imports vs. exports and inflation analysis.
  • GDP comparisons, regional distribution and country ranking views.

Skills demonstrated: data preparation, KPI reporting, comparative analysis, report layout and interactive visualization.

PROJECT 02 · MICROSOFT EXCEL

Superstore Sales Analysis

A one-page retail management dashboard built to explore sales, profit, customer segments and regional performance.

ExcelPower QueryPivot TablesPivot ChartsSlicers

Dashboard highlights

  • Total sales, total profit and profit percentage.
  • Regional sales performance and monthly trends.
  • Customer segment and category comparisons.
  • Interactive filters for Region, Segment and Category.

Business objective

Turn raw retail transactions into a structured dataset and an interactive management view for understanding sales performance, profitability and customer segments.

Data preparation & technical work

  • Inspect data quality, remove duplicates and correct data types.
  • Standardize inconsistent text and category values.
  • Merge Orders with Returns and People datasets.
  • Create Adjusted Sales, Adjusted Profit, Profit Margin and Sales Category fields.
  • Exclude returned orders from adjusted sales and profit calculations.
  • Build Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts and slicer-based filtering.

Analytical skills

Data cleaning, transformation, calculated fields, profitability analysis, trend analysis, segmentation and interactive Excel reporting.

Dataset: Sample – Superstore 2019.

PROJECT 03 · POWER BI

Superstore Sales & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive Power BI reporting solution with separate executive and product-level views for performance monitoring.

Power BIDAXPower QueryData ModelingDrill-through

Technical implementation

  • Import, clean and transform data using Power Query.
  • Build data model relationships and configure date hierarchy.
  • Create DAX measures for Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Running Total and Year-over-Year Growth.
  • Create calculated columns for Profitability Category and Shipping Delay.
  • Use interactive slicers, dynamic filtering and drill-through.

Two report perspectives

Executive Analysis: monitor overall sales, profitability and performance trends.

Product Analysis: explore product and category performance and profitability patterns.

Business value: help decision-makers move from transaction-level data to clear performance indicators and product-level exploration.
